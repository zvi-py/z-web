## ✦ Editorial & Motion Design System

1. **Editorial Typography Hierarchy**:
   - **Display Headlines**: `Inter Display` & `Syne` with precise tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) and uppercase structural rhythm:
     * *"WE TURN COMPUTATIONAL INSIGHT INTO SYSTEMS WITH SUBSTANCE."*
     * *"SELECT DIRECTORY TO NAVIGATE."*
   - **Monospace Metadata Indices**: `Fragment Mono` / `Suisse Int'l Mono` for tags, section indices (`[01]`, `[02]`, `[03]`), metadata stamps, and certificate verification ledgers.
   - **Editorial Body**: `Inter` with optical line heights (`1.65`) for maximum legibility and editorial rhythm.

2. **Caesar Burgundy & Obsidian Palette**:
   - **Deep Caesar Burgundy**: `#6D001A` / `#B32D47` (signature brand token).
   - **Pure Obsidian Surfaces**: `#000000` & `#141414` with light-theme contrast modes (`#FFFFFF`).
   - **Hairline Structural Borders**: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` and `rgba(238, 237, 228, 0.12)`.

3. **Motion Architecture**:
   - **Lenis Smooth Inertial Momentum Scrolling**: Physics-based lerp momentum deceleration on all viewports (`lerp: 0.085`).
   - **3D Fingerprint Scene Dock**: As you scroll down on the landing showcase, the 4 directory folders glide smoothly into the viewport centered over the 3D fingerprint macro hand (`top: 1650px`).
   - **Unified Folder Physics**: Spring-loaded folder hover animation (`scale(1.10) translateY(-14px)`) with Caesar Burgundy glow and cubic-bezier easing.
   - **Interactive Typewriter Hero**: Dynamic quote reveal sequence *"Some are meant to admire the stars. I was born to become one"* transitioning into a smooth `SCROLL` pill.
